Detailed owner-build evidence

Talon 3 29er × BBS02

The owner describes an easy conversion after a bike dealer removed the original bottom bracket. The build used a 44T chainring. Removing the front derailleur required breaking the chain, and the hydraulic brake sensor needed a custom magnet position. The owner reported a total bike-plus-kit cost below $1,400 at the time.

What remains unresolved

Giant has used the Talon name across many years, wheel sizes and component packages. Current and archived product pages may say only “cartridge” bottom bracket without publishing shell threading and width. The detailed owner’s 73 mm measurement is therefore useful for that frame, not a family-wide manufacturer specification.

Evidence limit: we can say an older Talon 3 29er with a reported 73 mm shell was successfully converted. We cannot say every Giant Talon 3 accepts a BBS02.

What to verify before purchase

  • Exact model year and wheel-size version.
  • Threaded shell versus press-fit and measured shell width.
  • Motor housing clearance at the chainstays.
  • 44T or alternative chainring clearance and resulting chainline.
  • Brake-sensor type and mounting method.
  • Battery clearance and secure mounting points.

Does a Bafang BBSHD fit a Trek Marlin 5?

Completed conversions exist, but the recurring 73 mm shell issue means this should not be described as a simple universal drop-in fit.

EVIDENCE GRADE B− · MULTIPLE BUILDS, RECURRING CAUTION

Reported fit, often with 73 mm installation compromises

Multiple pages establish completed Marlin 5 BBSHD conversions. Other reports describe spacer, bolt-length and lock-ring limitations when a 68–73 mm motor is installed in a 73 mm shell. Exact outcomes depend on frame generation, motor variant and chainring.

Why the 73 mm shell matters

Trek’s archived specification lists a sealed 73 mm cartridge bottom bracket. Reports about BBS-series motors on 73 mm shells repeatedly mention longer M6 screws, a 5 mm spacer at the motor plate and loss of the secondary outer lock ring. A purpose-made 5 mm spacer vendor makes the same warning about longer screws.

Do not infer: a motor entering the shell does not prove a secure installation, acceptable chainline or adequate thread engagement.

Pre-purchase checklist

  • Confirm the exact BBSHD shell-width variant being sold.
  • Measure the frame shell rather than relying only on model name.
  • Determine whether both lock rings can be installed.
  • Check required motor-plate spacers and M6 screw engagement.
  • Model chainring offset and cassette chainline before ordering.
  • Verify battery clearance for the actual frame size.

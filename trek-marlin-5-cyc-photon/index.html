Does a CYC Photon fit a Trek Marlin 5?

The available evidence supports a reported fit, but not a universal guarantee across every generation and frame size.

EVIDENCE GRADE B · MULTIPLE OWNER REPORTS

Reported fit on at least two Trek Marlin 5 builds

Two independent owners published completed Marlin 5 conversions using a CYC Photon or Photon Gen 2. One identifies a Gen 1 bike and lists a 38T chainring and CYC A52L battery. The other shows a completed Marlin 5 build but does not establish model year or frame size.

Bottom-bracket evidence

Trek’s archived Marlin 5 specification lists a sealed 73 mm cartridge bottom bracket for the referenced generation. CYC states that Photon options cover BSA 68/73/83 mm shells, as well as other widths and BB92 variants. That dimensional overlap is encouraging, but it does not prove chainstay, chainring or battery clearance on every Marlin 5.

What you must measure before ordering

  • Bottom-bracket shell width and inner diameter on your actual frame.
  • Frame generation and model year; “Marlin 5” alone is not precise enough.
  • Chainstay clearance for the chosen Photon housing and chainring.
  • Available battery space, especially on smaller frames.
  • Chainline after selecting chainring size and rear cassette.
Verdict boundary: this page supports “multiple owners report successful installations.” It does not support “all Trek Marlin 5 bikes are compatible.”

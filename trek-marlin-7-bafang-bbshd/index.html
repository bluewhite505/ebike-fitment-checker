Does a Bafang BBSHD fit a Trek Marlin 7?

Several completed builds support the pairing, but chainring clearance and chainline reports differ between frames and years.

An owner reports completing a Marlin 7 BBSHD installation in about four hours after a bike shop removed the original bottom bracket. Later discussion in the same thread documents chainring rubbing and imperfect chainline on at least one configuration.

Manufacturer dimensions and the unresolved part

Trek’s archived Marlin 7 page lists a VP BC73, 73 mm threaded cartridge bottom bracket. That supports selection of a BBSHD variant intended for the relevant shell range. It does not answer whether a specific chainring clears a particular year, wheel-size/frame-size combination or whether the resulting chainline reaches every cassette sprocket.

Open Trek specification ↗
Conflicting detail: one build reports no thrown chains, while another build thread discusses chainring contact and poor chainline. Both can be true on different years, sizes or chainring setups.

What to verify

  • Exact model year, frame size and wheel-size version.
  • Bottom-bracket threading and measured shell width.
  • Motor-width variant, spacer stack and lock-ring engagement.
  • Chainring model, tooth count and inward offset.
  • Lowest-gear chainline under frame flex and motor load.
  • Battery retention and triangle clearance.
